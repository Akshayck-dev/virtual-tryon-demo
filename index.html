How it works

Three steps. No app install, no webcam calibration — just a photo and AI.

1

Pick a garment

Choose from the demo catalog — shirts, tees, jackets, hoodies and denim.

2

Upload your photo

A clear, front-facing, full-body photo works best. It never leaves your browser except to the AI.

3

AI dresses you

A generative image model fits the garment onto your body — drape, folds, lighting and all.
